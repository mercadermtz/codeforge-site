In production

Our own product

Sonora.

An AI voice agent that answers the phone for small service businesses — clinics, trades, legal and property management — during the hours nobody can pick up. It books against the business's real calendar, takes messages, answers the questions the owner configured, and hands the call to a person when the caller asks for one.

Calls are inbound: members of the public dialling a business that engaged us to answer its phone. Each business controls its own number, greeting and configuration, and any transfer goes to a line that business nominated. We do not place unsolicited marketing calls.
